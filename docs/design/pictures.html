Dispatch

The Shuttle writes a task and does not wait. The owner starts each Thread from the board, and a task may have several. The board is derived from the files.

Marks

Drawn like the Loom wordmark: one stroke, 3.2 units on a 32-unit grid, round caps and joins, no fill. The SVG source is in the glossary document.

Loom

Existing. Warp and weft meeting at a corner.

Shuttle

The weft carried across the warp: over, under, over, and on. The three warp threads echo the Loom mark.

Thread

One strand going over and under. Its colour is its state.
